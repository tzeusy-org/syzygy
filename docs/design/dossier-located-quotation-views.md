## Proposed requirement delta

[Inferred] Replace the raw-display sentence in REQ-polaris-generation-034 with the following contract. Every quotation otherwise admitted to an existing operator-agent reader surface SHALL be located again at render in the verified pinned blob using the existing exact locator, including its line-range and contiguous-match checks. Syzygy SHALL retain the chosen substring of the blob's tracked normalised text from that same locator invocation and the corresponding original span. The reading view SHALL show the chosen substring as a quotation and disclose that syntax was normalised. The evidence view SHALL show the located original span byte for byte as escaped inert text. Both SHALL identify the same original byte range through one existing RFC7-10 anchor. Stored offsets, a second normalisation of the clipped original span, and the draft's text shall have no authority to supply either view.
