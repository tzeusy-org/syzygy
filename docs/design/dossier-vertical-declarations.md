## Inventory and draft

[Inferred] A versioned inventory form can propose up to twelve candidates. Each has an inventory-local identity, a short name, a scope paragraph and one or more existing path-and-line citations. Candidate names and scopes are Inferred authoring evidence. Keep the inventory produced and checked in its existing independent session. Its candidates inform the draft and fidelity review. The inventory contributes no renderable body or route directly, and a match between the two documents does not establish a vertical as Observed.

[Inferred] The future supported draft form has a `verticals` record with at most twelve entries. Each entry has a draft-local stable identity, a short name, one scope paragraph, one or more existing pinned path-and-line citations, and one decision with a reason. The three decisions are `expand`, `cover` and `skip`. They mean that the author proposes a sub-dossier, has assigned coverage within the parent, or has decided to omit additional coverage for the stated reason. A `cover` entry names the parent section that addresses it, validated against the draft's own produced sections. Identity and row order are for traceability and review. Scope and reason are Inferred. Neither the inventory nor the draft supplies a child URL.

[Inferred] An assessed empty set is represented explicitly with the author's reviewable explanation. It states the assessment in this draft. The denominator for the repository's actual feature verticals stays Unknown. A missing, unsupported or withheld declaration state follows the existing draft layer's treatment. The brief explains these states, the reviewer's comparison with independent candidates and the fact that a decision to expand is a plan. The adoption decides the exact field and brief versions after the predecessor composition is fixed.

## Check and fidelity

[Inferred] Check uses the frozen draft record as the sole source for declared identities, decisions and expansion totals. It rejects a duplicate or malformed identity, missing citation, invalid decision or reason, unmatched `cover` section, or more than twelve declarations. It rejects an `expand` total over the effective `verticalLimit` recorded for that run. Count validly declared expansion decisions before applying source visibility to the rendered rows. Check refuses when a supported declaration needs a limit and the effective run bound is unavailable. The run supervisor owns the declaration and lifetime of that bound. A depth-2 expansion guard belongs to `syzygy-ea4w.2.2`.

[Inferred] Use existing pinned path-and-range checking, source admission, screening and withheld treatments. The check reads only sources it could already cite and read. A structurally missing or invalid citation remains a named repair finding. A row supported only by screened-out sources is withheld through the existing claim rules, with only policy-permitted metadata. Screen draft-authored names, scopes and reasons through the existing claim-body rule even when the citations are admitted. The row's identity, authored name, scope, reason, excluded bytes and secret-matching paths do not appear in rendered output, link labels, indexes or outward diagnostics. A withheld row still contributes to the check's expansion bound where its decision is valid. A visible mixed-evidence row follows the existing withheld-count and provenance rules, with no secret-matching path. An entirely withheld draft supplies no declaration output. The independent fidelity review accounts for supported draft entries, divergent inventory candidates and assessed-empty explanations using the existing frozen-subject and review rules.

## What the parent shows

[Inferred] Within the parent section assigned to pillar 5 by `syzygy-ea4w.1`, draw one ordered list in draft order. Each visible entry shows its name, scope, decision and reason beside its evidence marking and citations. A `cover` row can link to its admitted local section. A `skip` row keeps its reason and needs no child link. An `expand` row describes a proposed expansion while the child is unavailable. Where the sibling delivery supplies a verified association to a rendered child at the same pinned revision, the parent can link to that child. The declaration renderer consumes that association. Run creation, depth enforcement, child URL allocation, breadcrumbs, status and spend belong to `syzygy-ea4w.2.2` and `syzygy-ea4w.2.3`. A reason text, section name or display title never supplies link authority.
