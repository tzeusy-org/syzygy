## Figure data and visual meaning

[Inferred] A produced figure has a stable figure identity, title, caption and existing home. An ordered list of labelled categories or samples defines the horizontal order. Series and samples carry stable local identities. Each value binds exactly one series/sample pair, carries a unit and declares its evidence state. Shape validation rejects missing or duplicate identities, extra keys, duplicate pair values, inconsistent units, invalid numbers and a declared size exceeding the future schema's finite bounds. Use exact finite decimal equality for evidence. Display rounding and tick placement are layout choices, never evidence equivalence. The reviewed specification must give literal limits on labels, samples, series, total marks, magnitude and precision and exercise the edge of each. A producer can omit a chart with a reason when valid material exceeds these bounds. A renderer reports any undisplayed population using permitted metadata, with Unknown where even its count is unestablished.

| Kind | Figure data | Drawing and reading order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bar | One series and ordered categories with one value or Unknown per category. | One category per position and a shared zero baseline. A signed value extends toward its own side of zero. A verified zero stays visible as a labelled zero. |
| Stacked bar | Ordered categories and a fixed order of additive, disjoint components in a shared unit, with the full expected component set for each category. | Signed components extend from shared zero in separate directions. Segment labels and states remain individual. Endpoints express the side's sum only when every relevant component is available. An incomplete category shows its known components and Unknown entries in its text form, without a geometric stack implying a complete total. |
| Line | Ordered samples with a common declared axis basis and a fixed order of series. A numeric or temporal sample coordinate increases strictly. A categorical axis states that spacing represents order. | Connect successive available samples within each series. A missing sample breaks the line. Points carry their own evidence states, and a connecting segment represents continuity of the drawn samples only. No interpolation or forecasting. |

[Inferred] The figure's quantities use a common unit in the axis and text form. Mixed dimensions require distinct figures. The author supplies a short caption stating the measure and relevant scope. Comparable marks share a scale, and every bar scale includes zero. A stacked bar requires a declared additive basis. Where evidence does not establish the components' disjointness, the derived total is unavailable. A total derived from a complete disjoint population is Observed only if each contributing value and its basis is Observed. If the population is complete and disjoint and any component is an estimate, the computed total is explicitly Inferred. The future checker must fix both the deterministic ordering and the allowed metric basis, including how it represents an absent sample. An unassessed value renders Unknown without a numeric mark. ## Numeric evidence at check and render

[Inferred] Use two bounded witness routes. The first reads one exact admitted numeric span at the pinned revision, checks its identity and integrity, and independently resolves the associated measure, scope and unit from the same admitted evidence. The second consumes an already-permitted deterministic Syzygy count with a named rule/version, declared input population and proven completeness at that revision. Its verified record supplies the count, unit and scope. A claimed partial count can be Observed when the exact partial population is itself positively identified. It must carry that denominator and its limit. Neither route accepts a self-authored digest, drafts' own labels as source identity, file-location coincidence, a repeated number elsewhere in the span, a freely supplied script or an arbitrary query. Do not reopen source bodies to fill evidence gaps. Preserve the current screening policy and use only existing authorized reads and derived artifacts. [SEC-3 and SEC-5](../../.syzygy/governance/doctrine/security.md).

[Inferred] The same numerical text can mean different things. A witness must bind value, exact unit, metric, category or sample and series meaning, using a closed mapping of supported measurements. Compute the value under the pinned rule/current evidence evaluation and compare it to the declared value after exact decimal interpretation. Reused artifacts retain their subject, revision, rule and evidence digest. If the necessary evidence is missing, stale, ambiguous, withheld, incomplete for the claimed scope, malformed or mismatched, check reports a repair finding at the exact claimed Observed value. The finding names the safe figure/value identity and a closed reason. It carries no unadmitted text or path.

[Inferred] At render, independently rerun the same bounded witness decision using the pinned input, as-of instant and admissible artifacts. A stored past verdict does not establish a current Observed value. If rederivation fails, the presentation and machine form both carry the existing permitted Unknown reason and withhold that value. A separately declared, permitted estimate stays Inferred even when it lacks a citation. An author cannot turn a failed Observed witness into an estimate as a render fallback. A genuine verified zero is an exact numeric claim. A missing witness or a missing sample is Unknown. [VIS-2](../../.syzygy/governance/doctrine/vision.md).
