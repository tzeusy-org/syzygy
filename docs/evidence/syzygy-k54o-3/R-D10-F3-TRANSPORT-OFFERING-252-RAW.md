## Findings

2. **Make completion depend on the actual send and response of the final part as well as the earlier parts.** [Observed] Baseline 025, proposed line 37, requires separate actual send attempt, send outcome and provider receipt events. [Inferred] That permits divergent implementations at the final boundary, including one that recognizes the full population and admits verdict based on a checked and pre-audited request before a durably linked successful send and admitted response. Require, in order, exact protected tool receipt, authenticated request, durable prepared-send audit, associated actual attempt and outcome, and durable uniquely linked complete admitted response for each depended-on transfer, including the request carrying the last part and producing the verdict call. Bind raw and prepared request identities, response and call identity, current role/attempt bindings and event integrity into the completion path. Specify the check before releasing a next-part call or admitting the verdict effect. Later review-check and render should revalidate historical evidence and perform a fresh local currentness check, since neither has to be a new inference request.
