## Findings requiring correction

### F1 — Material warrants were removed while the behavior they govern remains

[Observed] Candidate 034's body explicitly relies on RFC7-2, RFC7-9, RFC7-10, RFC7-13, RFC7-33 and RFC7-34 (lines 131–154), but its machine-readable contracts declaration (lines 341–349) lists none of them. Its `Source` still names RFC7-2. Candidate 035 explicitly preserves the fresh-reader review of RFC7-25 (line 363), but its declaration at lines 480–488 omits that clause. The predecessor declarations did name these material contracts.

[Observed] The same replacement declarations drop material product decisions and parent requirements. For example, 034 still normatively cites the rich-elements, voice/scripts and questionnaire directions and reads 002/003/004/006/012/019, yet its decisions list is only D10 and its sole parent is 033. 035 still governs and explicitly reads 006 but declares only parent 033. 036 explicitly reads 030 and 031 but declares only parent 033. The six class fields are present; their presence does not establish material completeness.

[Observed] CC-SPEC-2, in the supplied specification policy at lines 100 onward, requires: “Every requirement names all material governing warrants.” It also states: “This declaration is the only home.” Its rule does not permit an operative prose citation or another requirement's declaration to substitute for the requirement's own declaration.

[Inferred] This is a high-priority traceability failure. Replacing the warrant blocks with a common D10-oriented list hides continuing governing constraints and product decisions. The frozen `defined-contract-clauses.md` supplies only the six contracts now declared; it therefore also does not permit an independent fresh review of the omitted material contract definitions. No claim is made here about the unseen clauses' full contents.

Required correction: rebuild each requirement's six-class declaration from its actual retained and changed obligations. Preserve applicable predecessor warrants, retire only genuinely displaced ones with an explicit reason, and add D10/current doctrine where material. Supply the actual governing definitions needed for the corrected independent review. Do not solve this by deleting retained behavior or treating navigation-primary as sufficient.
