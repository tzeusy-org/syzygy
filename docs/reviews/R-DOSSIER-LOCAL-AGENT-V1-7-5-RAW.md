## Findings

**Finding 1 — A review check's measure against either limit is still unstated, so the design-repair limit's last revision, and at a repair-cycle limit of 0 the whole design-repair path, can be read as unreviewable** (revise)
Criteria 3, 6.
- Anchor: proposed spec line 23 ("a check or review check beyond the repair-cycle limit, a draft check being beyond it as REQ-polaris-generation-034 counts it, other than … a review check of that revision or of pages rendered from it (REQ-polaris-generation-034), which it SHALL refuse beyond the design-repair limit"). Line 245 (REQ-034's count, which counts revisions only). SEMANTIC-DELTA.md "Cases the counting rule decides, stated" ("Inventory checks, and review checks of a revision that counted against the repair-cycle limit, stay under REQ-033's v1.6 refusal beyond that limit; review checks of a design-repair revision and its pages fall under the design-repair limit instead").
- [Observed] The repair gives a measure to draft checks only ("a draft check being beyond it as REQ-polaris-generation-034 counts it"). REQ-034 counts revisions, not review checks, and REQ-035 (lines 522 to 532) ties no review round to either limit. No sentence says when a review check is "beyond" the design-repair limit, or "beyond" the repair-cycle limit.
- (a) Design-repair limit. [Inferred] Round 4's readings carry over unchanged to the limit the review checks were moved under. Read "beyond" as "once the limit is spent", the reading round 4 named for the repair-cycle limit: the design-repair revision that spends the limit is admitted at its check, and then its fidelity and rendered-design review checks are refused. With a design-repair limit of 1, the one design repair the operator allowed can never be reviewed, so close can never end "closed" on it. Read "beyond" as "a review of a revision checked beyond the limit", nothing is ever refused. The falsifier's new disjunct tests refusal "because the repair-cycle limit is spent" only, so it does not decide between these readings.
- (b) Repair-cycle limit of 0. [Inferred] Review checks of the first draft revision are not carved out, so they stay under "beyond the repair-cycle limit" with no measure. The first revision counts against neither limit (line 245), so the delta's case list, which names "review checks of a revision that counted against the repair-cycle limit", does not sort it either. A design-repair revision exists only after "the rendered-design verdict of record … on the latest pages rendered from that revision has an unresolved blocking finding" (line 245). That verdict needs a review check of revision 1. Under the "once spent" reading, a repair-cycle limit of 0 refuses that review check, so no design-repair revision can ever arise. That is round 4's own consequence ("With a repair-cycle limit of 0 the design-repair limit is never usable"), reached one step earlier. The brief's question, whether a design-repair revision and its review checks proceed past a spent repair-cycle limit, including 0, while within the design-repair limit: for the revision's own check, yes, determinately; for its review checks, yes as to the repair-cycle limit; whether the run can reach a design repair at all under limit 0, and whether the last permitted design repair's review checks run, depends on the unstated measure.
- [Inferred] No reading weakens a rule: every reading refuses more, never less. But round 4 rated this class revise under criterion 3 for the repair-cycle limit, and the repair reproduces it for the design-repair limit and leaves the first revision's case open. Part (b)'s ambiguity for review checks of the first revision is v1.6 text; it now decides whether the new P1 (b) mechanism can run.
- A repair, one clause: measure every review check by the revision it reviews, e.g. "a review check being beyond a limit only when the revision it reviews, or from which its pages were rendered, was checked beyond that limit", and a matching falsifier disjunct ("a review check is refused for a limit within which the revision it reviews was checked").

**Finding 2 — The falsifier's new first disjunct condemns the refusal of a design-repair revision's check at a spent design-repair limit, which the requirement mandates** (revise)
Criteria 6, 3.
- Anchor: REQ-034 Falsifier, line 507 ("a draft check is refused while REQ-polaris-generation-034's count against the repair-cycle limit is below that limit"). Line 245 ("Syzygy SHALL refuse a resubmission's check when the revisions already counted against its limit equal that limit"). Scenario "Design repairs drawn from their own limit", line 444 ("refuses the check only when that limit is spent"). Scenario "Zero repair and question limits accepted", line 75 ("the first resubmission that counts against that limit … is refused").
- [Observed] "Draft check" appears only on lines 23 and 507 and is defined nowhere. On line 23 the check of a design-repair revision is a draft check carved out of the repair-cycle measure ("other than the check of a design-repair revision"). Line 507 carries no carve-out and no cause.
- [Inferred] Counterexample. Repair-cycle limit 2, design-repair limit 0. Revision 1 passes its check and is rendered. The rendered-design verdict of record has an unresolved blocking finding, and no fidelity verdict of record does. The agent resubmits. Line 245 makes revision 2 a design-repair revision whose check must be refused (0 counted equals the limit 0). The repair-cycle count is 0, below 2. So the disjunct reads that mandated refusal as a falsification: the falsifier contradicts the requirement and two scenarios. A test built from the falsifier as written fails a correct implementation.
- [Inferred] Secondary, shared with the older disjunct "a design-repair revision is refused while the design-repair limit is unspent": neither names its cause, so a lawful refusal after the deadline, or for a consent or credential failure, also reads as a falsification. The second half of the new disjunct does name its cause ("because the repair-cycle limit is spent"), so the two halves are not parallel.
- A repair: "a check of a draft revision other than a design-repair revision is refused for the repair-cycle limit while REQ-polaris-generation-034's count against that limit is below it".
