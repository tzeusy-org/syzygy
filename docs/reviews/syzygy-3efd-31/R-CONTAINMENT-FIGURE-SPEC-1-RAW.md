## Findings

1. Blocking. A carried 2.5 sentence forbids the style rules a containment-only page needs. [Observed] Line 76 (carried unchanged from 2.5): the stylesheet displays exactly one of the two forms "by a container size query on that box" and "These style rules SHALL be carried by a page on which a produced figure of either kind [sequence or swimlane] renders its two forms, and by no other page". Line 126 requires a containment's `figure-forms` box to show exactly one form "by the container size query version 2.5 states", then "These style rules SHALL be carried by a page on which a produced containment figure renders its two forms, and by no other page". Scenario 2 (line 1266) speaks only of "the outline's style rules". [Inferred] On a page holding a containment and no sequence or swimlane, the 2.5 rule that hides `.figure-drawing` below W is required by line 126 and forbidden by line 76; on a page holding a swimlane and no containment, if the containment's display rule shares 2.5's selectors, line 126's "by no other page" forbids what line 76 requires. Two implementations (shared rules vs. duplicated rules) both read as conforming and differ in page bytes, which the oracle (line 1314) fixes. Repair: amend the line 76 sentence (declared in SEMANTIC-DELTA) or add an explicit sentence in line 126: "the `figure-forms` container query rules of version 2.5 are carried by a page on which a produced sequence, swimlane or containment figure renders its two forms, and by no other page; the `figure-outline` rules by a page on which a produced containment renders, and by no other page"; narrow "These style rules" in line 126 to the outline's rules; add a scenario (or extend scenario 2) for a containment-only page and a swimlane-only page.

2. Blocking. What a group deeper than level 4 raises is contradictory. [Observed] Line 120: `containment-depth` "at the path of each group at level 4 ..., a group below it raising no finding of its own"; scenario line 1271: "no finding for any group below it". The same paragraph: "For each group's `unit` Syzygy SHALL report the `unit-reference` finding"; line 122: relation-witness decides "the witness state of each nesting" and check reports `relation-challenged` "for each `challenged` nesting". [Inferred] A level-5 group with a malformed `top-level-directory` unit, or a level-4/level-5 nesting that is challenged, is required to raise a finding by lines 120/122 and forbidden by line 1271; it is also unstated whether the level-4 group itself still draws `unit-reference`, and whether nestings into or below level 4 are witnessed at all. This is exactly the depth 3 vs 4 adversarial case. Repair: state the scope literally, e.g. "a group below level 4 raises no `containment-depth` finding; `unit-reference` is reported, and nestings are decided, only for groups at levels 1 to 3 (or: for every group whatever its level)", and make scenario line 1271 say "no `containment-depth` finding for any group below it" (or name every kind it excludes).

3. Note. Containment findings alongside `schema` findings on the same list are undecided. [Observed] Line 120: "A `groups` list of fewer than one ... is a `schema` finding at that list", and `containment-flat` is reported "for a containment no group of which has a child", both at `$.diagrams[<i>].groups`. [Inferred] `groups: []` vacuously has no group with a child, so the text requires both findings at one path; likewise a six-member `groups` or a nine-member `children` (schema) with 41 groups (size). 2.5's precedent (line 72) suppresses a `schema` finding where a `figure-kind` finding stands, not the converse. Repair: say whether the containment findings are computed for a diagram whose `groups` or any `children` list has a `schema` finding (suggest: `containment-flat` only for a `groups` list of one to five members), and add the empty-`groups` case to the Form case list.

4. Note. Whether check decides nestings in a draft with a non-schema containment finding is unstated. [Observed] Line 100 (2.19, carried): check decides "the states of every edge of a v9 draft that has no `schema` finding". Line 122 says "every case of the order version 2.19 states applies to it unchanged" but the gate is in line 100's paragraph, not the order. [Inferred] A v11 draft with a `containment-depth`, `-size`, `-flat` or `figure-kind` finding (none of them `schema`) has an uncomputable layout (line 126, fit lists it Unknown) yet would still have its nestings decided and `relation-challenged` reported. Repair: one sentence stating which containment findings stop nesting decisions (suggest: decided only for a containment with none of the four kinds of finding, and in a draft with no `schema` finding).
