## Findings

1. **Blocking.** Location: the amended reading-ledger line, "a presentation element counts none; since version 2.15 a section's own prose takes in each of its subsections' answer `text` and plain blocks, for this count and for the RS-19 advice alike", against the added paragraph "A draft in schema v7 or earlier SHALL be checked and rendered exactly as version 2.14 checks and renders it, the same findings and the same bytes of every page, of `machine.json` and of every record check writes, except that a `field-version` finding stands in place of the `schema` finding at a section's `subsections`."
   Problem: [Observed] the ledger is written "for every draft that parses", including a v7 draft that carries `subsections` (it parses and gets the `field-version` finding). [Observed] Under 2.14 its `proseWords` count only `paragraphs` (`reading-ledger.ts`, `containerWords`; `reader-advice.ts:154`). [Inferred] The amended sentence is not gated on schema v8, so it makes that v7 draft's ledger count the subsection words and can raise a new RS-19 advisory, which changes the `readingLedger` bytes of `checks/rev-<n>.json`. That is the very record the v7 paragraph says SHALL keep 2.14's bytes. The two SHALL statements contradict each other for that population.
   Repair: gate the amendment, e.g. "since version 2.15, in a schema-v8 draft, a section's own prose takes in ...", and add the case to the Falsifier ("a v7 draft carrying `subsections` whose ledger or reader advice differs from version 2.14's").

2. **Non-blocking (design completeness; one item threatens acceptance criterion 2).** Location: `design.md` D8, "Every equality test on `LOCAL_DRAFT_SCHEMA_VERSION` that means 'has figure kinds' becomes that predicate ...: `figure-fit.ts:91`, `check-derive.ts:440` and `:503`, `render.ts:644`, `review-reach.ts:154`", and the rest of D8.
   Problem: [Observed] a grep of `LOCAL_DRAFT_SCHEMA_VERSION` and of the literal `polaris-dossier-local-draft-v7` over non-test sources finds sites D8 omits, each of which breaks a v7 or v8 draft once the constant becomes v8:
   - (a) `draft-schema.ts:47`: `hasThesisBasisForm` is `version === LOCAL_DRAFT_SCHEMA_VERSION_V6 || version === LOCAL_DRAFT_SCHEMA_VERSION`. A v7 draft would lose its thesis-basis form, so `check-derive.ts:364` would report `field-version` for `thesisBasis` and `render.ts:842` would drop the thesis line. This alone changes every stored v7 draft's findings and bytes.
   - (b) `reading-ledger.ts:52`: `HOME_SCHEMAS` is a literal set of v5, v6 and v7. A v8 draft's figures would be placed by `sectionId` (absent), so the ledger would be wrong.
   - (c) `reader-advice.ts:185`: the literal `'polaris-dossier-local-draft-v7'`. A v8 sequence would lose its RS-17 message advice.
   - (d) `check-derive.ts:444` and `:446`: the field-version `detail` strings interpolate `LOCAL_DRAFT_SCHEMA_VERSION` ("needs draft schema ..."). After the bump, a stored v6-or-earlier draft carrying a sequence or swimlane member would get "needs draft schema polaris-dossier-local-draft-v8", a byte change to its findings and check record, contrary to the v7-or-earlier paragraph and to acceptance criterion 2.

   - (f) `render.ts` `containerTitle` (line 655): a section's title becomes `Withheld item` when all its paragraphs and diagrams are withheld. The spec's "wherever a rule speaks of a section's paragraphs or its content it holds for its subsections' answers and paragraphs" requires it to take in the subsections' claims, and D8 does not say so.
   - (g) D8 does not say that `collectBlocks` and the new short-label entries in `elementLabels` walk subsections **only for a v8 draft**. Ungated, a v7 draft carrying `subsections` would get new label, citation and quotation findings, contrary to "every other finding version 2.14 reports" (see finding 1 for the same class).
   - (h) "identities of subsections and answers in the uniqueness check" is already true: the identity `walk` in `deriveFindings` visits every object of the draft. That is redundant, not wrong.

3. **Non-blocking.** Location: `design.md` D7, "no provider draft can carry `subsections` (its closed schema in `packages/polaris-generation-core/src/provider-draft.ts` has no such member)", and D8, "`packages/polaris-generation-core/src/draft-model.ts` gains the optional member and walks it in `draftHandles`".
   Problem: [Observed] the provider draft's closed schema is not in `provider-draft.ts`. It is the `draft` object exported by `draft-model.ts` (line 24), imported by `provider-draft.ts` (line 3) and used for the `author`, `edit` and `repair` stages (line 40). The two sentences together are ambiguous. [Inferred] If "gains the optional member" is applied to the `draft` closed schema, a provider draft could carry `subsections`, and any provider prompt or schema bytes derived from it would move, against the provider-golden criterion.
   Repair: in D7, correct the location. In D8, say that only the `DossierDraft` TypeScript interface gains the optional member and that the closed `draft` schema in `draft-model.ts` is unchanged. Optionally, add a test that the provider stage schema refuses `subsections`.

4. **Non-blocking.** Location: rule `one-sentence-answer` version 1, "the text is one sentence when what remains ends with a full stop, a question mark or an exclamation mark, alone or followed by one closing parenthesis, ... and holds no full stop, question mark or exclamation mark that a white-space character follows."
   Problem:
   - (a) [Inferred] There is a false negative: a terminator followed by a closer and then white space is not a sentence end under this rule. `The daemon caches reads (on start.) It never writes.` passes as one sentence, and so does `It is 'done.' Then it stops.` D4 lists only false positives.
   - (b) [Inferred] "White-space character" is undefined (ECMAScript `\s`, Unicode `White_Space` and ASCII differ, for example at U+0085 and U+180E). So are the terminators: is U+2026 or U+3002 a full stop? The same applies to the trim step. Two conforming implementations can disagree.
   - (c) [Inferred] Text with no terminal punctuation at all passes when it ends with a closed quotation (`It is "fast"`). That may be intended, but it is not stated.

   Repair: name the code points (U+002E, U+003F, U+0021; white space as the RS-19 word split defines it, or as an explicit list), and either also count a terminator followed by one or more of `)`, `]`, `'` or U+2019 and then white space as a sentence end, or list the closer false negative in D4 and D10 as a known residual. Add the parenthesised two-sentence case to the literal-case tests either way.

5. **Non-blocking.** Location: "`answer` one claim block in the paragraph form the introduction takes (never a presentation element), whose `text` is one sentence", with "A subsection's answer ... are claims of the section"; and the brief paragraph, "`answer` is one claim block, with `id`, `label`, `text`, `citations` and `quotations` as a paragraph has".
   Problem: [Observed] The paragraph form (`claimForms`, `draft-schema.ts:144`) admits a `non-normative` block, which "is no claim", as well as `basis`, `executionIds`, `reason` and `judgement`. The spec does not say whether a non-normative answer is allowed. If it is, an "answer-first" subsection can open with no claim. The brief's member list omits `basis`, which is required for an Inferred block, and `reason`, which is required for an Unknown one, so an author following it literally gets a `schema` finding.
   Repair: state whether the answer may be `non-normative` (recommend not: "an Inferred or Unknown claim block", with a finding kind for a non-normative answer, or narrow the schema arm). Reword the brief to "in any form a paragraph takes (with `label`, `basis` or `reason`, `text`, `citations` and `quotations` as that form requires)".
