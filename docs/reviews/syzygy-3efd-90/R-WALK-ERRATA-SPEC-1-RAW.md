## Findings

1. **Blocking.** `openspec/changes/dossier-walk-errata/proposed/polaris-generation/spec.md`:300 (the appended sentence "the label and marking such a tooltip carries stay shown by the node's box in the drawing and by its item in the figure's list"); same claim in `design.md`:40 ("The node's box in the drawing still shows its label") and `design.md`:43 ("loses a duplicate of text already on the page, not information"); row A-344 ("stay shown by the drawing's box and the figure's list").
   - Problem: [Observed] the drawing does not always show the label. `wrapLabel` (`apps/three-surface-poc/src/polaris-generation/diagram-layout.ts`:311-329) wraps a node label to `nodeChars` = min(22, (nodeWidth−24)/8) characters (`:480`) and at most 2 lines (`:483`), and replaces the rest with `…`. For any label longer than that, the `<title>` tooltip was the only place in the figure that showed the full label, and on a walked figure it is now hidden. [Observed] The figure's list holding the full label is inside a collapsed `<details><summary>Read every component and relationship</summary>` (`dossier-site.ts`:961), so "stay shown" overstates it: the label stays *available* one disclosure away. A linked box's hidden text carries the full label to assistive technology but is not visible to a pointer user. The change exists to make the spec state shipped behaviour honestly, and this normative sentence states something false for elided labels.
   - Repair: change the spec sentence to say that the drawing shows the marking and the label, or its first lines ending in an ellipsis where the label is longer than the box holds, and that the full label and marking stay in the figure's list of every component and relationship (behind its disclosure). Mirror the wording in design.md D3 (and say the elided-label case is the real cost a pointer user pays: one disclosure to open), and in row A-344's summary. Rebuild by script, regenerate the delta, rebind the digest. Accepting the trade-off can stand; only the description of what is lost must be exact.

2. **Note.** `design.md`:34 ("the rule 2.50 states for a zoom-only figure's empty boxes") and `design.md`:44.
   - Problem: [Observed] 2.50's pass-through rule (spec.md:312, "inside a `zoom-only` wrapper the layer and every box let the pointer through to the drawing, and only a `zoom-link` takes it") applies to every box of a figure that has no walk, not to "empty boxes"; and a `zoom-link` is a small "+" set at its box's top right, which takes the pointer over that mark. So "A figure with no walk keeps its tooltips" is true except over each zoom mark, and the second clause ("so does a box version 2.50 lets pass the pointer") names boxes that only exist on figures with no walk, so it adds nothing beyond the first clause.
   - Repair: in D3 describe 2.50's rule as "every box of a zoom-only figure passes the pointer; only its zoom link takes it", and either drop the redundant spec clause or make it precise ("…keeps its tooltips, save over a zoom link's mark"). Not blocking: the deviation is a few pixels and 2.50 is not yet implemented.
