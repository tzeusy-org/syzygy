# PWB extraction when a repository uses a different document grammar

Decision for `syzygy-sgey` under VIS-4. Basis is protected Syzygy commit `a060d3e015b215fddc9aee2abe670fadbd7d640d`.

Retain source-local, class-wide grammar failure for PWB. A grammar failure makes the item denominators for that class in the source, the source, and the affected class and category aggregates Unknown. Keep successful sibling classes and successful sources in the evaluation with their own evidence. For an account of a foreign repository, select the separately governed, operator-started local dossier with a pinned clone, declared provider, committed screening and independent review. Its dossier claims follow their own sources and review. PWB counts continue to follow the PWB evaluation.

[Observed] PWB-REQ-002 requires an Unknown denominator for an unreadable class in a source and explicitly rejects a partial population from that failed class. It admits other classes in the same source that read successfully. A loaded profile is its project's sole ruleset and uses the closed shapes and forms. Butlers uses the built-in literal grammar until it declares a profile. If the declared profile is unread or refused, the denominators stay Unknown and no items are emitted. Other projects also have Unknown denominators and emit no items without a loaded profile. P-86 and N8 are the signed bases for those rules. PWB surfaces an unenumerated heading with its anchor and route. The declared headings keep their items, and the unenumerated heading mints none. The affected source, class and category denominators are Unknown because of that heading. When every class reads, the source remains extracted. The current POC runtime also binds observation to its configured approved locator and Git common directory. [Inferred] Retaining class-wide failure protects the provenance of literal extraction when ambiguity affects item boundaries or duplicate identities. It reduces modeled coverage when a document the operator cannot change violates its grammar. A local dossier can describe repository-specific structure through independently reviewed evidence under the dossier gates. This routing accepts the lower PWB coverage and leaves its denominators explicit.

[Observed] A-53 and REQ-polaris-generation-035 define the local operator-started dossier and its disclosure and history-isolation rules. The URL supervisor is governed separately. PWB reads remain subject to PWB-REQ-005/006 and SEC-5. Syzygy observes repository content inertly, and any separate execution of project code by an agent follows SEC-3 and the owner decision boundary in VIS-4.
