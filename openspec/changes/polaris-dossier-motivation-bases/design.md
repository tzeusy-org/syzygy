# Design for motivation bases and external comparisons

## Inputs and report boundaries

[Observed] The operator-agent [base](../polaris-dossier-local-agent-mode/specs/polaris-generation/spec.md)
keeps the source revision pinned, verifies the dossier's spans in REQ-034,
and binds the fidelity verdict to the packet in REQ-035. The
[operative REQ-034 successor](../dossier-containment-figure/proposed/polaris-generation/spec.md)
retains that provenance and the dossier's reader topics. REQ-032's
[non-governed profile](../polaris-non-governed-narrative-profile/specs/polaris-generation/spec.md)
defines separately recorded profile rules. This draft consumes applicable
rules and locally available checked spans.

The author identifies the kind and basis of a claim. The checker can
establish the marking, locate a verified source and render the citation
and trail. The independent verdict determines whether the source text
states the point or the trail supports the reconstruction. The quotation
span can be Observed. Authorship inferred from path classes and claim
semantics assessed by review remain Inferred. Where the statement and
reconstruction disagree, the record preserves both with their respective
bases.

External source labels are the agent's declaration, screened under SEC-5.
They carry no source page, citation anchor or Observed quote. The checker
refuses marked external blocks while 10b stands. The fidelity verdict
detects unmarked external reliance and blocks readiness. If external
presentation becomes eligible through a recorded reversal, it remains
outside the opening and concise account. The verdict accounts for that
report under the current packet as `external-unverifiable`. The report
receives no accuracy or support credit. The eligibility condition is
rechecked at render. The disclosed transmission remains the agent's
report of activity outside Syzygy's observed record.

## Review controls

Use a pinned synthetic tree with an authored statement, a generated
snippet, paths exercising each third-party arm, and a code-and-test pair
whose behavior supports a reconstruction. Vary the marking, the trail and
the review's exact subject. Include a commit-message premise and a vague
Unknown disclosure, then compare renderer labels, records and readiness
against literal expected values. Keep the third-party test distinct from
the SEC-5 source-screening decision.

Use separately authored refusal, reversal, withdrawal and unreadable
governing-record fixtures for REQ-039. Exercise an unmarked reliance and an
external block placed in the opening. Compare human and machine output and
capture Syzygy network activity independently. These are candidate oracle
inputs for review and later implementation.
