# Polaris dossier motivation bases and external comparisons

This candidate implements the drafting step of
[the November 3 answer C](../../../.syzygy/governance/decisions/OWNER-DIRECTION-QUESTIONNAIRE-ANSWERS-2026-11-03.md#3-arbitrary-public-repo--answer-c)
for syzygy-cnwp. Adoption and runtime work are pending.

## Why

[Observed] The [October 7 direction](../../../.syzygy/governance/decisions/ARBITRARY-PUBLIC-REPO-DOSSIER-DIRECTION.md)
asks for an honest reconstruction when a project has sparse documentation
about why its maintainers built it. The November 3 answer splits that
authoring behavior from the standing repository permission. Ruling 10b
continues to require maintainer-stated advantages.

[Inferred] A reader can use a reconstruction if they can inspect its
premises and see where the author's reasoning starts. The same distinction
lets an independent reviewer reject an inference that the source fails to
support.

## Scope

[The proposed delta](proposed/polaris-generation/spec.md) adds two
requirements for already admitted operator-agent runs. REQ-038 requires a
basis for definite motivations, trade-offs, advantages and positions. It
binds a maintainer-stated block to a verified quotation or a reconstruction
to checked premises and an independently reviewed trail. Missing evidence
can still be disclosed as Unknown.

REQ-039 keeps the 10b refusal operative. It describes the conditional
presentation of an agent's external report after a separate recorded
reversal. The report's source labels carry no evidence authority and
Syzygy makes no external request. The standing permission,
history admission and the predecessor packet's questions stay with
[the parked change](../polaris-dossier-arbitrary-public-repo/proposal.md)
and syzygy-mzge.
