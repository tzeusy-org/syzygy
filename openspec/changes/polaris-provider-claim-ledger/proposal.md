# Polaris provider claim ledger

This candidate covers syzygy-u05.13's claim ledger and the evidence state of
each generated sentence. It is a draft for independent review under CC-REV-2.

## Why

[Observed] The September N13 pursuit records a weaker generator intermediate
than the kit's claim ledger and an inability to recover a sentence's computed
evidence state. The provider discovery map currently validates a blob handle
and claim text per entry. That shape alone supplies neither the exact support
and qualifications for the interpretation nor a durable association for the
reader. [N13 pursuit](../../../docs/pursuits/2026-09-22-vision-pursuit.md)
and [provider response grammar](../../../packages/polaris-generation-core/src/provider-draft.ts).

[Inferred] One versioned intermediate gives an author and a fidelity reviewer
the same inventory of project statements, support and qualifications. A
reader can tell what each generated assertion relies on and where a
qualification limits it. This serves VIS-1 and VIS-2 and preserves the
deterministic observation and inference boundary in VIS-7.

## Changes

The [proposed delta](proposed/polaris-generation/spec.md) supplies complete
successors for REQ-polaris-generation-003 from the adopted base and
REQ-polaris-generation-019 from the adopted understanding overlay. Their
existing source citation rules continue to govern. One block citation can
support several sentences where each association is recoverable.

The draft covers the provider intermediate, sentence evidence projection
and compatibility with earlier supported provider records. The scored
corpus, example envelope and quickstart remain on the original outcome.
The operator-agent dossier, edit-stage proposal on syzygy-dov.23 and effect
permissions keep their separate governing records.
