composition stages. The adopted generator's schema contract governs anchor
identity and the separation of presentation from authority. The adopted
understanding overlay governs versioned research projections. Human and

issues scoped entry identities and resolves already-admitted references.

through their versioned inputs. Existing presentation paths project the

## Identity and evidence

A ledger binds one draft revision and one admitted input snapshot. An entry
carries its claim identity, statement, intended meaning, support or typed
unavailability, qualifications and conflict disposition. A relationship
carries the identities at its ends, kind, direction and evidence
disposition. The underlying artifact and evaluation identities stay on the
support records. The ledger records the author's interpretation.

The author supplies explicit sentence occurrence-to-entry associations.
The validator checks coverage of project assertions, including those in
headings or captions, and verifies every association belongs to the
same revision. Punctuation in a source literal or a formatted identifier
does not itself create an assertion. Non-factual controls and connective
wording use existing roles. Structural validation checks encoded references.
The reviewer judges semantic coverage, including whether factual text is
classified as framing or a sentence outruns the stated support.

Inferred identifies a generated assertion with sufficient reconciled
evidence. Unknown carries unresolved or unavailable evidence and its
permitted reasons. A direct source quotation keeps its captured tuple as
a distinct unit. Other tuple fields keep their governing meanings. An
adjudicated scope qualification can coexist with Inferred when the sentence
honors it. A qualification that leaves the sentence unsupported produces
Unknown.

A reader gets the state and any Unknown reason alongside the assertion in
text. Supporting detail stays reachable through the existing attribution.
A block citation shared by two sentences can appear once while each
sentence still resolves its own support.

## Verification and compatibility

Use one synthetic snapshot with an anchor that supports two assertions,
one withheld input and a conflicting claim. Compare the sentence identities,
labels, reasons and anchor bindings in both reader forms against
independently specified expected records. Include a generated factual
heading and a non-factual navigation label to test coverage. Verify the
fidelity review binds the ledger of the exact draft revision. Read an older
supported artifact through its declared compatibility path and compare its
bytes before and after.

Mutate duplicate identities, missing and cross-revision sentence
associations, dangling edge endpoints, mixed evidence and producer claims
of authority. Exercise bounded size and unavailable-source fixtures under
existing controls. Identical admitted inputs at a fixed recorded revision
give the same validation and presentation projection. A later revision
binds its own record. Existing bounded diagnostics, retention and
cancellation govern the stage.

[Inferred] The schema contract's record roles, asset types, block-local
support and identity rules are consistent with the draft. The executable
grammar, stage and review bindings, version negotiation and both
presentations would move together in an adopted implementation. A version
and compatibility reader boundary are assigned with adoption.
